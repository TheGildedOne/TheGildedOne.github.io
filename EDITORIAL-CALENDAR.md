### Why this order

The first six posts are all Mystery Cults, deliberately. A new site does better
establishing depth in one topic before spreading out — it gives Google a clear signal about
what the site is *for*. The pillar goes first so everything published after it has
something to link back to.

The two biggest traffic posts (Alexandria, Eleusis) are placed where they benefit from
some existing internal links rather than launching cold.

## Sustainable pace

3x/week is aggressive for one person writing sourced material. If it starts to slip,
**drop to 2x/week rather than dropping quality.** Consistency matters to Google; volume
much less. A site publishing two solid posts weekly for a year will beat one that
publishes thirteen good ones and stops.

The month-one archive is evergreen. None of it dates, none of it needs updating, and all
of it keeps working while you write the next batch.
